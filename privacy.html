Privacy Policy

Rocket Blast — Temir Game Studio
Last updated: October 7, 2026
Android package name: com.temirgamesstudio.rocket

This Privacy Policy explains how Temir Game Studio collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use Rocket Blast.

For questions or privacy requests, contact info@temirgamestudio.com.

Website: temirgamestudio.com

Information We Collect

Player account information: Rocket Blast uses an automatically created anonymous player account. Your required nickname, automatically generated account identifier, and internal player identifier may be associated with your account. Nicknames are unique across the game. We recommend that you do not use your real name or other personal information in your nickname.

Leaderboard information: Your nickname, rank, highest altitude, total Coins earned, and certain flight statistics may be processed to operate the leaderboard. A country indicator may also be used. Country information may be derived from approximate network location or regional device settings. Rocket Blast does not access GPS or precise location.

Local game information: Game progress, virtual currency balances, items, upgrades, Market transactions, Daily Login status, and device preferences may be stored on your device. Not all locally stored game data is sent to the server.

Gameplay and technical diagnostics: In test or development builds where diagnostics are enabled, gameplay sessions, flight results, fuel use, rocket configuration, control use, virtual currency earned or spent, and similar gameplay activity may be collected. Device model, operating system version, display characteristics, graphics settings, and performance measurements may also be processed. Detailed gameplay diagnostics are not enabled in the current standard Android release configuration.

Service error information: Our infrastructure providers may log technical errors and service events to identify security issues and operational problems.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate Rocket Blast and its online features; manage player accounts and unique nicknames; provide leaderboards; support game progress, Market, and Daily Login features; verify account ownership; and prevent abuse or unauthorized activity.

Where diagnostics are enabled, gameplay and performance information may also be used to identify technical issues, understand gameplay interaction, and improve game balance.

We do not use this information to create personalized advertising profiles.

Authentication and Player Accounts

Rocket Blast uses Supabase anonymous authentication to create player accounts. You do not need to register with an email address or password to play.

The automatically generated account identifier is used to associate online player data with the correct player and help secure the account. Your nickname is your public display name and is not a login credential.

“Anonymous authentication” does not mean that all game data is anonymized. Online records may remain associated with the automatically generated player account. Rocket Blast does not use the advertising identifier as the identity of your player account.

Gameplay Analytics

Certain gameplay statistics may be sent to the server to operate the leaderboard. In test or development builds with diagnostics enabled, more detailed gameplay, economy, and performance information may be collected for analysis and balancing.

In the current standard Android release configuration, detailed diagnostic sessions, flight records, and economy events are not sent to the server. This information is not used for personalized advertising tracking.

Third-Party Services

The current version of Rocket Blast uses Supabase for backend services including anonymous player accounts, online data storage, leaderboards, remote game configuration, account/data deletion, and—where enabled—game diagnostics.

Supabase Privacy Policy

The current application version does not include an active advertising network, Google Play Billing for real-money payments, Google Play Games integration, or Play Integrity integration. Coins and other in-game virtual resources are not real money or real-world currency.

If these services change in the future, this Privacy Policy and relevant data disclosures will be updated as appropriate.

Data Sharing

Information may be processed on our behalf by service providers that supply technical services necessary to operate Rocket Blast.

When you use the leaderboard, your nickname, rank, country indicator, and certain game achievements/statistics may be visible to other players. Your authentication account ID and internal player ID are not displayed to other players.

Information may be disclosed when required to comply with a legal obligation or lawful request. The current application does not send player information to advertising partners.

Data Retention

Player data may be retained for as long as necessary to provide Rocket Blast services or until you delete your account and data.

After account deletion, active player records linked to your account are removed from our game systems. Limited technical verification records may be retained temporarily to ensure that deletion is completed safely and idempotently; these records do not contain your nickname or gameplay history.

Infrastructure logs, security records, or backups may remain for a limited period under service-provider retention policies or applicable legal obligations.

Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your player account and associated data from Settings inside Rocket Blast. An internet connection is required. If the server cannot verify deletion, the operation is not considered complete and your existing local game data is preserved.

A successful deletion removes the player/authentication account, nickname registration, leaderboard record, related server-side game and economy records, available gameplay sessions/flight records/feedback, and player-specific Market records stored on the server.

After the server operation succeeds, the local player account and game progress are also cleared. Rocket Blast then returns to the new-player flow with a new anonymous account and nickname. Device preferences such as language, audio, and controls may be retained.

Account and player-data deletion cannot be undone.

Web deletion request: temirgamestudio.com/delete-data.html

If you need assistance, contact info@temirgamestudio.com and identify Rocket Blast and, if available, your player nickname. We may request additional information to verify account ownership. Do not send passwords, session tokens, or other secret authentication information. Because Rocket Blast does not create player accounts using email addresses, an email alone may not be sufficient to identify your game account.

Security

Rocket Blast's configured online services use HTTPS/TLS for data in transit. Player-specific operations and in-game deletion requests are checked through an authenticated player session.

Although appropriate technical measures are used to protect information, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children and Teen Privacy

Rocket Blast is offered to players aged 13 and older. We recommend that players do not use real names, contact information, or other personal information in public nicknames.

The anonymous player-account system does not by itself technically verify a user's age. If you are a parent or guardian and have a request or concern about data associated with a child's use of Rocket Blast, contact info@temirgamestudio.com. We may request additional verification information to safely identify the relevant player account.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Rocket Blast features, services, or data-processing practices change. When material changes are made, the updated policy will be published, the “Last updated” date will be revised, and additional notice will be provided where required.

Topladığımız Bilgiler

Zorunlu takma adınız, otomatik oluşturulan hesap kimliği ve dahili oyuncu kimliği hesabınızla ilişkilendirilebilir. Takma adları oyun genelinde benzersizdir. Takma adınızda gerçek adınızı veya başka kişisel bilgilerinizi kullanmamanızı öneririz.

Liderlik tablosu bilgileri: Takma adınız, sıralamanız, en yüksek irtifanız, toplam kazanılan Coin miktarı ve bazı uçuş istatistikleri liderlik tablosunun çalışması için işlenebilir. Liderlik tablosunda ülke göstergesi de kullanılabilir. Ülke bilgisi ağ altyapısının sağladığı yaklaşık coğrafi bilgiden veya cihazın bölgesel ayarlarından elde edilebilir. Rocket Blast GPS veya hassas konumunuza erişmez.

Yerel oyun bilgileri: Oyun ilerlemesi, sanal para bakiyeleri, eşyalar, geliştirmeler, Market işlemleri, Günlük Giriş durumu ve cihaz tercihleri gibi bilgiler cihazınızda saklanabilir. Cihazınızda saklanan her oyun verisi sunucuya gönderilmez.

Oyun ve teknik tanılama bilgileri: Tanılama özelliğinin etkin olduğu test veya geliştirme sürümlerinde oyun oturumları, uçuş sonuçları, yakıt kullanımı, roket yapılandırması, kontrol kullanımı, kazanılan veya harcanan sanal para ve benzeri oyun etkinlikleri toplanabilir. Bu sürümlerde cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi sürümü, ekran özellikleri, grafik ayarları ve performans ölçümleri gibi teknik bilgiler de işlenebilir.

Mevcut standart Android yayın yapılandırmasında bu ayrıntılı oyun tanılaması etkin değildir.

Hizmet hata bilgileri: Çevrim içi hizmetlerimizin çalışması sırasında oluşan teknik hatalar, güvenlik sorunlarının ve hizmet problemlerinin tespit edilmesi amacıyla altyapı sağlayıcılarımız tarafından kaydedilebilir.

Takma adınız görünen oyuncu adınızdır ve giriş bilgisi olarak kullanılmaz.

“Anonim kimlik doğrulama”, oyun verilerinin tamamen anonimleştirildiği anlamına gelmez. Çevrim içi kayıtlarınız otomatik oluşturulan oyuncu hesabınızla ilişkilendirilebilir.

Rocket Blast, reklam kimliğini oyuncu hesabınızın kimliği olarak kullanmaz.
